## Research & Projects

### Self-Supervised Learning for Biomedical Imaging

Exploring **self-supervised representation learning** for biomedical imaging, including DINO-style training with Vision Transformers.

My current work investigates how useful representations can be learned from **unlabeled biomedical images** and subsequently evaluated for downstream biological and clinical tasks.

`Biomedical Imaging` · `DINO` · `Vision Transformers` · `PyTorch` · `Self-Supervised Learning`

---

### Topology-Aware GNNs for 3D Porous Titanium Scaffolds

Developing a computational pipeline for predicting **stress and strain fields in generative 3D porous titanium scaffolds** using topology-aware graph neural networks.

The project combines:

`Procedural Geometry` → `Finite Element Analysis` → `Graph Construction` → `GNN Field Prediction`

Work includes:

- Procedural generation of **triply periodic minimal surface (TPMS)** structures
- Tetrahedral finite-element simulations
- Mapping FEA stress/strain fields onto graph representations
- Automated dataset construction and quality control
- Training and evaluating GNNs with **PyTorch Geometric**
- Node-level prediction of **von Mises stress** and **equivalent strain**

`Graph Neural Networks` · `PyTorch Geometric` · `FEA` · `CalculiX` · `Scientific ML`

---

Worked with **NTK Labs at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU)** on research involving **advanced motion capture (MoCap) systems for use in hospital settings**.

I worked specifically on **validation procedure creation and baselining**, helping establish methods for evaluating system performance and creating reference measurements for subsequent testing and analysis.

`Motion Capture` · `Biomedical Technology` · `Validation` · `Data Analysis`· `Biomechanics`
